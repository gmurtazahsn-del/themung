Who We Are

Welcome to THE MUNG.

THE MUNG is an independent game-studio project built around creativity, experimentation, and learning technology from the ground up.

We want to turn ambitious ideas into playable experiences, explore unusual concepts, and make each project a step toward something greater.

This is the beginning. The worlds will grow as we build.

01. Original Ideas

We start with imagination and look for ways to bring it to life.

02. Custom Technology

We aim to understand and create the systems behind our games.

03. Long-Term Vision

We build a foundation first, then expand toward bigger projects.
